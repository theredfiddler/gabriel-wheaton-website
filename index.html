Using his violin and effects pedals, Gabriel creates a rich orchestral sound palette ranging from fiery and engaging to mellow and relaxing. His expressive voice brings an improvisational mastery to folk, classical, pop, bluegrass, Latin, hip-hop, electronic, jazz and everything in between.

Gabriel was classically trained, graduating from UCLA Summa Cum Laude with a BA in Music Composition. He works in Northern and Southern California as a freelance composer, producer and violinist.

Gabriel has performed with notable artists including T-Pain, Mako, Elle King and Pomplamoose.
