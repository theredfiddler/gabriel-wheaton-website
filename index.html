Gabriel Wheaton

Violinist & Composer

About

Gabriel Wheaton is a versatile violinist and composer based in California, specializing in a unique blend of classical, folk, and contemporary music. With extensive experience across genres including classical, pop, bluegrass, Latin, hip-hop, electronic, and jazz, Gabriel brings innovative arrangements and virtuosic performance to every project.

Classically trained at UCLA where he received a BA in Music Composition (Summa Cum Laude), Gabriel combines traditional violin mastery with modern production techniques, often incorporating effects pedals to create dynamic, layered soundscapes that push the boundaries of what the violin can achieve.

Based in both Northern and Southern California, Gabriel is available for performances, collaborations, studio sessions, and special events.
